## 📌 Project Overview

HealthFirst Care, a multispecialty hospital, was facing severe operational bottlenecks — long patient wait times, frequent scheduling errors, and uneven resource utilization across departments. This capstone project simulates the end-to-end role of a Business Analyst tasked with diagnosing these issues and designing a data-driven digital transformation solution.

The project follows a complete BA lifecycle: requirements gathering, stakeholder analysis, process mapping (As-Is/To-Be), data analysis, dashboarding, and risk management — culminating in actionable recommendations for hospital leadership.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

- Average patient wait time: **41.4 minutes** (target: ≤ 33.1 min)
- **32 double-booking incidents** due to manual scheduling
- Only **26.8%** appointment completion rate
- **42.7%** of hospital resources unavailable or under maintenance
- Orthopedics department overloaded at **7.86 hrs/day** vs. a 3.85-hr average

## 🔑 Key Findings

1. **Manual processes are the primary bottleneck** — front-desk paperwork and manual availability checks drive nearly all wait time and double-bookings, not clinical capacity.
2. **Resources are unevenly distributed, not scarce** — Pediatrics runs at 4.85 hrs/day usage while General Medicine/Orthopedics hover near 3.4 hrs/day.
3. **Dissatisfaction clusters at predictable peaks** — Mondays, weekends, and the 6 PM hour see both the highest volume and lowest satisfaction.

## ✅ Key Recommendations

- Deploy an **automated conflict-prevention scheduling engine** with live resource-tracking dashboards
- Rebalance staffing toward peak-demand windows (Mondays, weekends, 4–6 PM)
- Rebalance clinical resources from underutilized departments toward Pediatrics
- Enforce a weekly risk-review cadence for the 7 high-severity risks identified

**Target Outcomes:** Wait time ≤ 33.1 min · 0 double-bookings · ≥ 70% resource availability · Satisfaction ≥ 8.0/10
